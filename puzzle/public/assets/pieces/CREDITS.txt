
Chibi Characters (c_student, c_short, c_merchant, c_archer, c_ninja, c_knight) are head renders of

c_kuro, c_pony, c_maskbob, c_maskT and c_pumpkin combine parts of the same Chibi Characters models; c_pumpkin's hat
is pumpkin_orange_jackolantern from KayKit Halloween Bits by Kay Lousberg (CC0).
